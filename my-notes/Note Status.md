## To Activate
There is something to do in that note.
## Active
I'm actively working and thinking about that
## Suspend
There is something to do, but for a reason I'm waiting for something else. 
## Inactive
There is nothing to do in the note, and I'm not actively thinking about that.
it doesn't mean that I'm not changing it. it simply means that I don't have any plan to work on it.
