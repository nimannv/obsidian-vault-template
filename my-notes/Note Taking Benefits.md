1. Maybe the main thing I want from my notes is to be a **good backup for what I have in my mind**. then:
	- When I want to continue thinking about something, **I won't start from the beginning**.
	- Make the knowledge I build up **easier to review**.
	- Make it easier to find those parts of my mind that I've forgot.
		Let's say I have an idea today and I'm taking note of it, but I can't work on it for a while, and it goes into the shadow! And there is no chance to retrieve that idea because I don't know which notes need review! (I try to solve this problem using [[Note Credit]])

2. The other benefit of note-taking for me is [[Writing-to-Learn]] something.
