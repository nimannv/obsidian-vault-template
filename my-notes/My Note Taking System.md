> [!note] What is it about?
> How my desire note taking system works

I've been inspired by [[Note Taking Frameworks]] that I've tried to use. (in some cases even by their name!)

So basically, what I'm trying to do is simplify and combine them together to make my own desired system.

## My Definitions

### Notes
Notes are the main blocks that build up the whole system.
They represent my mind blocks and link them to each other.
Everything in my mind is a note! Anything! An idea, a person, a company, a movie, or anything else in my mind. There is no separate directory or folder to organize them; they are all in one directory because they are all equally notes!

### Resources
Basically, they are anything out of my mind! They are separated because they are not in my mind as long as I create a note and link to them! I only have them to make them able to reference.
Let's say I bought a book and put it on my bookshelf. What does it mean?! nothing! So far, I just made it available for my mind! But it doesn't mean anything till I open it, read it, and it creates some ideas in my mind. And then after that, those ideas are not that book anymore! Those are my ideas, my understanding, and they are in my mind (linked to that book in my bookshelf).
Therefore, they are separated because I don't have any control over the ideas there. I don't create or edit them. I only add them and use them to create my notes.
