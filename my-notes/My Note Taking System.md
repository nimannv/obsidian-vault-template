I've been inspired by [[Note Taking Frameworks]] that I've tried to use. (in some cases even by their name!)

So basically, what I'm trying to do is simplify and combine them to make my own desired system and solve my [[Not Taking Challenges]]

> Maybe over time my system turns into one of those systems. (It doesn't have to be a unique custom system) The only thing that matters is I'm using a system that works for me doesn't matter what the name is!

## Main Components

### Notes
Notes are the main blocks that build up the whole system.
They represent my mind blocks (at a specific time) and their connections with other entities.
Everything in my mind is a note! Anything! An idea, a person,,a company, a movie, even a category of notes is a note itself! 
Anything in my mind should be considered a note. There is no separate directory or folder to organize them; they are all in one directory because they are all equally notes!
[[Note Rules]]

### Resources
Basically, they are anything out of my mind! They are separated because they are not in my mind as long as I create a note and link to them! I only have them to make them available to reference.
I don't create or edit them. I only add them and use them to create my notes.

> Let's say I bought a book and put it on my bookshelf. What does it mean?! nothing! So far, I just made it available for my mind! But it doesn't mean anything till I open it, read it, and it creates some ideas/notes in my mind/vault. And then after that, those ideas are not that book anymore! Those are my ideas, my understanding, and they are in my mind (linked to that book on my bookshelf).
