> Note Maturity definition. It shows how mature that idea is in my mind.

- baby: not sure if I have an idea; there is something, but I just write it down! All notes are baby notes in the beginning.
- young: I have an idea!
- adult: I have a clear idea.
- elderly: It became kind of a belief!
The more maturity, the lower the chance of change!
